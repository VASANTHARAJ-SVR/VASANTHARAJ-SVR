## 🧠 About Me

I build technology around **real business problems**.

My journey started with Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, but building products for real users changed the way I think about technology.

The goal is no longer simply to write better code.

The goal is to understand the problem, design the right system, ship it, measure the outcome, and continuously improve it.

Today, I lead **AINCURU**, an AI-first product development and business automation company focused on helping businesses turn ideas, workflows, and operational challenges into production-ready technology.

- **Long-term trust matters more than short-term hype.**
